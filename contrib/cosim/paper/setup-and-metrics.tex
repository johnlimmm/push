For transaction $i$, latency is measured from local session activation at R
to correction completion, $L_i=t_{\mathrm{corr,end},i}-t_{\mathrm{session,start},i}$.
We distinguish signed latency bias from mean absolute error:
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{MAE}_L=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}
\left|L_i^{\mathrm{model}}-L_i^{\mathrm{Full}}\right|.
\end{equation}
The expected fidelity is computed by enumerating the four Bell-measurement
outcomes in the independent quantum reference at each model's operation times:
\begin{equation}
\mathbb{E}[F_i]=\sum_{b\in\{00,01,10,11\}}p_{i,b}F_{i,b}.
\end{equation}
Expected-fidelity bias is the mean of
$\mathbb{E}[F_i]_{\mathrm{model}}-\mathbb{E}[F_i]_{\mathrm{Full}}$.
Decoupled predicts latency and deadline satisfaction only; no fidelity or
service-feasibility prediction is defined for that model.
Service feasibility uses the pre-specified $F_{\min}=0.5$ and a 5-ms latency
deadline. Deadline-only and joint fidelity/deadline misclassifications are
reported separately relative to Full Sync, not to physical hardware ground truth.
