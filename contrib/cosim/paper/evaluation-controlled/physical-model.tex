\paragraph{Parameter provenance and literature reference profile.}
Liao et al.\ identify nominal NV-based instruction times in
Table~3 of their NetSquid study, \emph{Benchmarking of quantum
protocols}~\cite{liao2022benchmarking}.
Iqbal et al., in \emph{Investigating Imperfect Cloning for Extending
Quantum Communication Capabilities}~\cite{iqbal2023cloning}, reuse these
times and explicitly include H, X, and Z in the single-qubit category
(Table~3). Their Section~4 also specifies the memory and gate-noise
parameters. We use this published profile as the reference for a
literature-based extension, rather than attributing the current
controlled settings to a calibrated device.

Under QuCl's serial CNOT--H--M--M schedule, the literature times imply
$T_{\rm BSM}=20+0.005+2(3.7)=27.405~\mu$s.
This is a circuit-derived duration, not a reported BSM measurement.
Using these times does not by itself reproduce a complete physical NV
processor; gate, readout, and memory assumptions must remain explicit.

A distinct trapped-ion model in \emph{Quantum data centres: a
simulation-based comparative noise analysis}~\cite{campbell2025qdc}
uses 135~$\mu$s single-qubit gates, 600~$\mu$s two-qubit gates, and
6-ms measurements (Table~1). Its measurement duration is an author
estimate, and its memory model is depolarizing. This provides a
published precedent for the 600-$\mu$s scale, but does not substantiate
our complete 600/200/400-$\mu$s instruction profile. All figures below
retain the controlled settings; evaluation under the literature profile
requires new runs and a workload designed around its service times.
